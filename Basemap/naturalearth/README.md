# Natural Earth

Made with Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain. Version 5.1.2 GeoJSON
from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector, fetched by `scripts/fetch-basemap.sh`:
coastlines, country borders, state and province lines and lakes at 1:50m, and populated places
at 1:10m, reduced to name, population and rank.
